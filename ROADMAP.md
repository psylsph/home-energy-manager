# Roadmap

Priority order for the open GitHub issues, as of 18 November 2026 (v1.20.0). Effort is a rough guide: **S** is a day or less, **M** a few days, **L** a week or more.

Every open issue is either waiting on someone outside (listed in Tier 0) or has a plan, linked in its row. The plans are local working notes in `docs/` (gitignored). The issues raised in the last two days come first (Tier 1: #472's 00:00 question is still open, the rest are built), then the older planned work. Within a tier, cheap items that are ready to build come before ones that still need a decision from Stuart.
