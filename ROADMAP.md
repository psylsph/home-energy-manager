# Roadmap

Priority order for the open GitHub issues, as of 24 November 2026 (v1.24.1). Effort is a rough guide: **S** is a day or less, **M** a few days, **L** a week or more.

Every open issue is either waiting on someone outside (listed in Tier 0) or has a plan, linked in its row. The plans are local working notes in `docs/` (gitignored). Tier 1 is the work that is next to build; the rest follow in order. Within a tier, cheap items that are ready to build come before ones that still need a decision from Stuart.
