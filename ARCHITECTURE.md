# Architecture Notes

Detailed behaviour and invariants per feature, kept out of `AGENTS.md` so they aren't loaded into every agent session. Read the relevant section before changing a feature, and update it in the same change when the behaviour changes.

## Frontend (`src/`)

Entrypoint `src/main.tsx`. **Pages**: Status, Battery, History, Forecast, Control (model-aware rate scaling, slot-labelling warnings), Inverter, Power, Solar, Meters (each CT clamp titled through `lib/meters.ts::meterLabel`: its name from Settings → Meters, else its Solar Arrays name, else `Meter 0x01`, with the address kept under a name; the same names label the Grid CT Meter and Solar Arrays pickers, issue #473), Octopus (hidden until configured), Settings, Logs (developer mode only).

**Components**: `EnergyOrbitDiagram` (radial SVG power flow), `house/HouseEnergyView` + `house/HouseIllustration` (optional isometric house view, issue #422), battery panels/gauges, charts, `ColdBatteryWarning`, `NavDrawer`; both Status diagrams build their view model through `hooks/useEnergyFlowViewModel` over `lib/energyFlow.ts` (status words via `nodeStatus`, battery `mode`/`socText` fields — never parse `unit`), and the house maps it with `lib/houseFlow.ts` onto `lib/houseGeometry.ts` — one cable per part through the inverter, as on an inverter's own power-flow screen (one line off the panels; battery, home and grid each one cable carrying their net power, coloured by the largest flow on it; the home cable runs to a consumer unit, no cable leaves the inverter's top, and the grid cable comes up the path and along the front wall, clear of the hedges since the routes draw over the illustration), so the two views always agree.

The house is drawn from `--app-house-*` variables in `index.css` (both themes, no SVG filters for the Pi); the weather corner adds Open-Meteo's `shortwave_radiation` as a solar-intensity gauge, five 200 W/m² bars with a matching word (`weatherIcon.ts::solarLevelWord`: None, Weak, Low, Moderate, Strong, Intense, issue #475) and the figure in the hover title and screen-reader label; Solar, Grid and Battery readings show volts/amps (`houseFlow.ts::electricalDetail`, the orbit's own unit/sub-label strings) in a bubble on hover, or on tap since phones have no hover; under it `house/HouseStatusCards` shows the running or next charge, export or discharge window (`lib/chargeSchedule.ts::batteryEvent`, on the inverter's clock with a browser-clock fallback when the snapshot has no `inverter_time`, merged like the Control page's windows) from every source: Timed Charge, the HEM-managed Timed Export schedule (`hooks/useTimedExportSchedule`, parsed by the shared `lib/timedExportSchedule.ts`; outside its windows the inverter sits in Eco and its slots may be cleared), the inverter's own armed discharge slots (Force Discharge, calibration, Agile, Timed Demand) and Timed Discharge in the pause registers; "…Now" uses the Control page's `telemetryConfirmsExport` for exports, and the battery icon pulses while it runs and the stored energy with SOC-coloured level bars; with Settings → Panel Controls → Show Car Charging Slots on (`status_show_car_charging`, offered with Octopus set up and the house view chosen), the schedule card adds the car's next planned Octopus charge as a second line (`hooks/useCarChargeSlots` over `lib/carChargeSlots.ts::carChargeLine`, browser-local times, touching dispatches merged across cars, hidden when stale, failed or empty; issue #474) that never enters `batteryEvent`; the card's label says `Tomorrow` when its next window starts after midnight (`ChargeWindow.tomorrow`), and a Planner line under the car line (`hooks/usePlannedCharge` → `GET /api/forecast/plan/upcoming`, `lib/plannedCharge.ts`, issue #481) says when the auto-apply or nightly refresh will rewrite slot 1 (`forecast/refresh.rs::upcoming_write` runs the poll loop's own gates, `once_a_day_gate` and `every_window_due`, forward a minute at a time so the two can't disagree), never what it will write: the plan may write a shorter slot inside the cheap period, so the card never shows a planned window and `batteryEvent` never sees the Planner; with Show Battery Runtime on (`status_show_battery_runtime` in settings.json, so every browser shares it), the Battery Level card (or, in the orbit view, a Battery line under the diagram; both `components/BatteryRuntimeRow`) says when the battery reaches its reserve (`hooks/useBatteryRuntime` → `GET /api/battery/runtime` → `forecast/runtime.rs`: the Planner's forecast payload from the live SOC at the current minute, simulated unfloored for the median, p75-load/low-solar and p25-load/high-solar days (for the rest of the current hour the median and high-solar days take the live solar where it beats the forecast, every day takes the live load where it beats the profile, and while the battery discharges the hour can't gain), predicted times rounded to 30 min and never back before now (`round_not_before`; the card also drops a time that has passed) while a charge's own start (a slot, or a #434 top-up at its dispatch's start) is said exactly, never a bare "at" for a prediction ("at about" instead): a range ("Empty tonight between 21:30 and 23:00", "at about" when the ends are within 30 min, "at 21:30 or later" when the quiet day never empties; the likely time clamped into it, `clamp_into_range`), one to three dots from the spread, "Lasts until the 23:30 charge" when a slot or solar refills it first and a busy day lasts too, else "Empty tonight at 21:30 or later, charge at 23:30" with dots from the gap (`RechargesFirst::risk`, the p75-load/low-solar day's empty time when before the refill; day words from the range's start, "tonight" from 18:00), "Full at about 04:00" (or the running slot's target) while charging, the inverter's slots plus HEM's Timed Export windows (a #434 top-up counts as a charge), "Discharge paused" while discharge is paused (reserve 100, or HR 318 by `hr318_blocks_discharge`), "At Reserve" at a lower one, or a point above it, where a cold battery really stops, and "Below Reserve" any point under the reserve itself (every forecast floors at reserve + 1, `simulate.rs::modelled_reserve`: the Forecast page's projections, the Planner and the runtime; planner wording still names the inverter's own reserve via `displayed_reserve`; a reserve of 100 is untouched; issue #479) (Title Case at Stuart's request, unlike the other states) (`runtime.rs::reserve_band`, also in the cache key), while two or more points under and not charging the house's battery cabinet and the card's percentage and level bars flash (`houseFlow.ts::belowReserve`, the same `is_charging` threshold, not for HEM's reserve-100 pause; `reserve-flash` in `index.css`, off under reduced motion), "Unable to calculate" (reason in the ⓘ) while charging faster than the forecast predicts or when the backend answers unknown; cached 5 min, or until SOC moves 2 points or the schedule, pause, battery direction (charging, idle, discharging), dispatch revision or forecast settings change, with concurrent requests sharing one build; issue #472).

With Today statistics hidden (`showTodayStats`, Settings → Panel Controls) the whole Status page must stay unscrolled at 390×763 (iPhone 12 Pro less safe areas), and with them shown it must fit the desktop app's default window (the size in `tauri.conf.json`, which the e2e check reads) in both views, with the battery runtime line on too (and the house view's car line), so the Today card has no separate heading (its bar titles say Today); the house view starts with them hidden — when no toggle value is saved, house hides the bars and the orbit shows them, and switching views re-applies that until the toggle is saved.

Choosing the house view (`energyFlowDisplay`, localStorage) swaps the bottom bar for `NavDrawer`; both list pages from `lib/navItems.ts::visibleNavItems`.

The Status page no longer shows `BatteryPanel` (it lives on the Battery page); Today statistics are `EnergyBalanceBars` over the six day counters (issue #429), built from the pure `lib/energyBalance.ts` view model — two stacked bars share one scale (the larger total, so a gap stays visible; fills grow by their share with a 3rem minimum so a tiny segment stays readable), each fill stacks its own label and value (the label wraps on narrow segments; no separate legend), AC-coupled systems and the residential All-in-Ones (no PV input) explain in the ⓘ that the separate PV isn't measured, and the summary leaves the solar share out, and a `balanceSummary` percentages line sits underneath

The `components/settings/` folder holds one component per Settings section (issue #402), built from `SettingsSection`/`SettingsGroup` chrome and the `useSettingsSave`/`useSettingToggle` save plumbing, with pure helpers in `lib/settingsForm.ts`. `SettingsPage` keeps only the layout, the toast and the single `/api/settings` fetch; sections mount after it and seed their forms from it, while Notifications, Weather and Remote Access load their own endpoints. Test a section by mounting it alone.

**Hooks**: `useWebSocket` — connects to `/ws`, reconnects on drop, fetches snapshot via REST. **Lib**: `api.ts`, `format.ts`, `types.ts`, `evcLabel.ts`, `validators.ts`. **State**: Zustand `useInverterStore` — snapshot, connectionState, developerMode (persisted), EV Charger state incl. the `evcEverConnected` latch distinguishing "charger was here, now offline" (`Disconnected`) from "never reached" (`Not Found`); `resetEvc()` clears it when the user saves a new host; `meterLabels` (meter names and solar arrays, loaded with the nav settings in `App.tsx` and refreshed by their Settings sections on save).

`?RO` read-only links hide Control, Settings and Octopus from the nav, and a typed address of one goes back to Status, from one list (`navItems.ts::hiddenInReadOnly`, used by `visibleNavItems` and `App.tsx`'s `page()`); visible pages wrap their write controls in `components/ReadOnlyHidden` (via `hooks/useIsReadOnly`, which also reads the `?RO` address so the first render agrees with the nav) (the Planner's Apply and Plan Settings, the Cosy banner's Control link), and `PageLink` (and the cold battery warning) falls back to plain text for a page the link can't reach, so in-sentence links don't bounce to Status (issue #471) — a convenience, not a security boundary. The frontend talks exclusively to the local Axum server — never directly to the inverter.

## Backend (`src-tauri/src/`)

- **`lib.rs`** — Tauri setup + headless CLI; spawns Axum server + Modbus poll loop. Two tracing layers: stdout `fmt` (WARN, `RUST_LOG` override) and `LogCaptureLayer` into the in-memory `LogRing` (runtime-adjustable via `PUT /api/log-level`).
- **`history/`** — SQLite (`~/.givenergy-local/history.db`); `HistoryDb`, time-bucketed `query_history()` (AVG, or MAX for cumulative fields), `insert_reading()` (raw row for seeding/imports). The poll loop records via `minute.rs` (issue #379): `queue_poll_reading()` → `flush_poll_rows()` on the blocking pool writes one row per wall-clock minute (power/voltage/temperature averaged; SOC and `today_*` from the latest poll), rewritten with the full aggregate when the next minute starts; `close_open_minutes()` writes the open minute on shutdown (`flush_history_on_shutdown`, bounded to 5 s headless). Invariants: rows land in poll order (queue taken under the connection lock); the writer replaces only rows it inserted itself and never overwrites another row at the same second, so existing history is never deleted (a restart may leave two rows in one minute).
- **`inverter/`** — `model.rs` (`InverterSnapshot`, `DeviceType` Gen1-4 / AC-coupled / three-phase / AIO / HV Gen3-4 / EMS, with model-aware slave addresses, poll blocks, slot counts, battery-protocol selection); `decoder.rs`/`encoder.rs` (register ↔ snapshot / `ControlCommand` → whitelist-validated writes); `sanitizer.rs` (corruption defense, see below); `poll.rs` (drain writes → read → sanitize → broadcast; dongle memory-leak fingerprinting, model-aware slave switching, carry-forward, LV battery protocol at 0x32+ vs HV at 0xA0→0x70+/0x50+); `discovery.rs` (network scan validating the 0x5959 magic header); `state_machines.rs` (connect/reconnect, battery protocol, tariff automation).
- **`modbus/`** — `client.rs` (`ModbusClient`: FC6 writes, stale-frame drain, dongle heartbeat echo, default slave `0x11`; `read_all_with_extras()` picks optional blocks by device type); `framer.rs` (proprietary MBAP + transparent sub-frame + CRC); `registers.rs` (addresses, poll blocks, safe-write whitelist, HHMM codec; per-battery BMS reads and HV stack reads polled separately from `STANDARD_POLL_BLOCKS`).
- **`server/`** — Axum: `api.rs` (REST), `ws.rs` (snapshot stream), `logs.rs` (`GET /api/logs`), `mod.rs` (router + graceful bind). EVC endpoints: `/api/evc/discover`, `/api/evc/status` (reachability + cached snapshot, seeds `evcEverConnected` on page load).
- **`evc/`** — EV Charger client (standard Modbus TCP port 502); broadcasts `PollMessage::Evc` per snapshot, `EvcConnected` on TCP handshake, `EvcDisconnected` on invalid host or connect failure (also clears `latest_evc` so `/api/evc/status` reports `reachable=false`).
- **`settings/`** — persisted JSON (`~/.givenergy-local/settings.json`).
- **`forecast/`** — solar forecast (issue #283): hourly Open-Meteo fetch on a 3h tick riding the weather loop (a failed fetch is retried after 15 min, `SolarFetch::next_fetch_in`), persisted to `history.db::forecast_values`; a forecast over 6 h old, or none left while weather is on, becomes 72 h of sunless hours (status `solar_assumed_zero`, issue #483) so the Planner still plans every cheap window and never relies on stale sun, while the export advice and back-stop floor stand down and the battery runtime counts the sun as unknown; a future fetch time still refuses to plan; self-calibrating site performance ratio (median daily actual-vs-insolation, ≥5 usable days); `consumption.rs` hour-of-day profile (median + p25/p75), with EV charging left out (issue #400), per day-hour because the home counter moves in 0.1 kWh steps (a minute's delta is 0, 0.1 or 0.2): the EV charger's recorded energy over the hour's intervals (`history/evc_power.rs`: the EVC poll averages each wall-clock minute into `evc_power_minutes`, merged by sample count) is subtracted, floored at zero, then hours the charger didn't cover end to end are clipped at the optional `forecast_exclude_load_above_kw` (a polled idle charger means a heavy hour really was the house); without a GivEnergy charger the Octopus dispatch log's energy comes off where the charger didn't record (issue #406, `excluded_dispatch_kwh`). Planned car charging (`forecast/dispatch_load.rs`): `dispatch_overlay` turns the fresh dispatch list into per-hour `ev_kwh` + `held_fraction` (`SimHourInput`; a held share imports instead of discharging, and pauses export), applied to the plan's `forecast_sim_hours` too; the car's load goes in only as far as the profile is free of it (`car_in_profile`: charger ≥ 95% of the history or a dispatch log unbroken since the history's start with believable energy → full; threshold only → the part above each hour's load; else hold only, status `dispatch_load_not_added`); `believable_kwh` (≤ 22 kW × duration) is shared by the planner and the profile. The payload's `ev_dispatch_windows` feed the Planner's "Next car charge …" line and `ev_dispatch_hours` its Car Charging bars; `simulate.rs` hourly battery SOC projection, and `current_schedule.rs` the "with current schedule" line from the inverter's slots plus HEM's Timed Export windows (`ForecastInputs::timed_export_slots`, from `server::api::timed_export_slots`; outside its windows the inverter's slots are cleared); `planner.rs` cheapest-window `PlanRecommendation` with forward wrap; `GET /api/forecast/plan` returns the plan plus an `apply` payload mirroring Control endpoints. `SolarForecastProvider` trait allows alternative sources.
- **`octopus.rs`** — Octopus account integration (issue #212): authenticated half-hourly import/export/gas intervals stored separately from inverter history (supplier data arrives late and may be corrected; gas units preserved as returned — SMETS1 kWh vs SMETS2 m³); powers `/api/octopus/*` (billing costs, HEM-vs-supplier comparison, CSV/PDF export). Cosy/Agile tariff automation is separate, riding the poll loop via `state_machines.rs`.
- **`octopus_dispatches.rs`** — Intelligent Octopus Go dispatch hold (issue #394), opt-in from the Octopus page: polls `flexPlannedDispatches` for the account's live EV devices every 3 min (token reused 50 min, device list 6h) and, while a *smart* dispatch is running by a list ≤ 15 min old, `DispatchHoldState` in `state_machines.rs` pauses discharge as a `DispatchHold` owner of the shared Eco Paused state (below Manual Force so Force Charge runs during a hold; Force Discharge, pauses (the Pause button, `/api/control/mode`'s paused modes, the external pause) and Timed Discharge are refused while it holds or an AIO capture is still to be restored; `Safety` when a limiter pauses too, issue #431). A pause the inverter won't take is retried every 5 min after two tries (`DispatchHoldRetry`), shown on the Octopus page and alerted once per dispatch (a later failure in the same dispatch doesn't alert again). On the single-phase All-in-Ones the hold pauses with HR 318 mode 2 (discharge only, so Force Charge and charge slots still charge; a charge-cap pause it replaces comes back with the restore) and an all-day window instead of reserve = 100 (`check_native_dispatch_hold`; only an existing all-day discharge pause is left alone — a part-day Timed Discharge window is paused over), capturing HR 318–320 in `Settings::dispatch_hold_native` before its first write (only from a fresh, valid read of an inverter with a serial; five minutes without a usable read before or during the hold, or with a capture that can't be saved, reports it failing; any HEM write to HR 318–320 marks that poll's read stale) and restoring them exactly (retried with the same back-off until a fresh read confirms, on the reserve path too, for 15 minutes at most: then HEM lets go, with an alert saying what to put back, so a restore that won't land can't own the battery or refuse commands for good; Resume Discharge during a restore never writes the reserve; the poll saves the hold's state before releasing its locks); it doesn't share the limiters' reserve pause. Boosts are never held; a failed or stale read means normal behaviour. The Status page's car line (issue #474) shares the hold's list while the hold is on; with it off, the loop fetches a display-only list into `AppState::octopus_dispatches_display`, which only `GET /api/octopus/dispatches` reads: it never holds, tops up, plans, replans, stamps rates or writes the dispatch log. `evaluate_pause_limiters()` runs temperature → dispatch → load so a dispatch starting as the load limiter releases keeps the saved reserve. Each successful check also logs the dispatches that have started (`history/dispatch_log.rs`, `octopus_dispatch_log`, issue #406) off the lock, never affecting the hold; a dispatch counts until one check after it was last seen, or its planned end once it leaves the list after it. Meta `octopus_dispatch_log_since` is when the log ran without a break: any gap over 30 min between writes (`octopus_dispatch_log_last_write`, so HEM being closed counts), a clock step, or the hold switched off restarts it; rows are never pruned (a re-price needs every smart charge, issue #434).
- **`inverter/plan_replan.rs`** + `forecast_plan::run_plan_replan` — re-plan charge slot 1 when Octopus moves a dispatch (issue #404). Each daily write (auto-apply or refresh) leaves a `PlanCycle` (`AppState::forecast_plan_cycle`, in memory): the slot, the window occurrence (local-time ends), and `DispatchesState::revision` the plan used (`plan_changed`: start, end, type, or energy by ≥ 1 kWh; `None` when planned without a fresh list). When the revision moves it recomputes: before the window as normal, inside it with `PlanInputs::running_window_start` (`OccurrenceAt::Running`: only tonight's occurrence, none left → `NoPlan`) and `ForecastInputs::include_current_hour`, only in the first 10 min of an hour. It rewrites only when the action, hold target or an end moves ≥ 15 min, waits for gates (Gateway, Adaptive owning the rate, sit-out, a #434 top-up, a stale list) with the change pending, ends the cycle when slot 1 reads back as another slot twice in a row, records plan history `replan`, and notifies only under auto-apply. `planner::plan_needs_current_hour` also gives the daily plan the current hour when the window starts later within it (IOG's 23:30 planned at 23:05).
- **`inverter/plan_outcome.rs`** + `plan_outcome::run_plan_outcome` — reports the level a planned hold charge actually reached (issue #507). Read-only: `PlanOutcomeWatch` (`AppState::plan_outcome`, in memory) follows charge slot 1 as read back while it has exactly the times and target the Planner last wrote (`PlannedSlot`, noted by `forecast_plan::queue_slot_plan` and the Planner's Apply in `set_charge_slot`; a full charge, a cleared slot or any other save of slot 1 replaces it, so a user's own schedule is never reported and a restart forgets it until the next plan) and, on the first poll after the window ends, logs the last reading inside it against the target (`info`, or `warn` when it ends more than 2 points under), with the target the inverter reported and, when HEM watched from the start and the battery had to charge, when it first read the target. One report per occurrence, made even when the slot already reads as the next window; a gap over 10 min inside the window keeps the end level but drops the peak and reached time, while a gap that runs to the end, a first poll more than 10 min after it, a clock stepped back, or a Gateway (its schedule copy says nothing about the battery) gives no report rather than a guess; a replan to another target is judged against the new one. It sends the text as a plan notification only under auto-apply, through the master alerts toggle like the other plan notifications.
- **`forecast/export_floor.rs`** — the export back-stop floor (issue #407, advice only): `export_floor_soc` walks back from the next cheap window's start over a pessimistic day (p75 load, never below the median, low solar band; `server::api::forecast_sim_hours_banded` with `LoadBand::P75`), adds each shortfall, takes off storable sun, clamps to [reserve, 100], adds a margin (5 points, 10 while `consumption_sufficient` is false) and floors at `max(planner minimum, reserve)`; bad input or reserve ≥ 100 → 100. `planner::plan_export_window` takes it as `ExportPlanInputs::backstop` and holds the pessimistic day's SOC at or above the per-hour need through the cheap window, so the floor always wins and the forecast never overrides it (misaligned hours → no export). Export Once and Saving Sessions (`inverter/export_once.rs::backstop_at_start`) raise the booked target only upward at the start of an export, from a fresh plan (20 s timeout, end time on the inverter's clock), skip it as Failed with a note when the battery is already at or under the floor, and keep the booked floor with a note when there's no import tariff or no usable forecast.
- **`inverter/plan_rate.rs`** — Spread Charge write-and-restore (issue #508, the write half of #483), behind `forecast_plan_spread_charge`. The Planner's auto-apply and refresh (`forecast_plan.rs::apply_plan_recommendation_with_spread`) write a spread plan's slot exactly as a hold plan (whole cheap window, the advice's target SOC, charge limit still at the full rate) and *arm* the window in memory (`AppState::plan_rate.armed`, never persisted: after a restart the charge simply runs at full power). The poll loop (`poll.rs::run_plan_rate`) lowers the limit only once charge slot 1 is running, after saving a run (`Settings::plan_charge_rate_run`: baseline, lowered value, window end) so the baseline is on disk before the write. It puts the baseline back at the window's end, or early when Force Charge, the #434 top-up, calibration, Adaptive Charge or a safety limiter needs the limit; a restore is confirmed by a later read, retried (two fast, then every 5 min) and given up after 15 min with one alert saying what to put back. Invariants: the saved baseline is the user's value, never HEM's lowered one; a charge-limit write that isn't HEM's, or a read-back that is neither the lowered value nor the baseline, ends the run unrestored (the user wins); a read-back still showing the baseline never ends a run (the lowered write may not have landed); a run for another serial is dropped, never restored onto another unit; graceful shutdown restores (bounded), and a run past its end at startup restores on the first poll. The Planner page's Apply button takes the same path: `GET /api/forecast/plan` puts the whole-window hold in `apply` with an `apply.spread` block (`api.rs::spread_apply_json`), and once the slot POSTs succeed the page posts that block to `POST /api/forecast/plan/arm-spread` (`forecast_plan.rs::armed_from_request` re-checks the setting, Adaptive Charge, the model, the rate and the window; a refusal is not an error for the apply, the charge just runs at full power). Anything that captures the charge limit as a baseline while a run holds it down uses the run's baseline instead (`plan_rate::baseline_charge_rate`: Force Charge, the #434 top-up, a calibration cycle, Adaptive Charge's saved limit), or it would put the lowered value back and strand it. The snapshot's `plan_charge_limit_back_at` lets the Control page say when the limit returns, instead of warning that the slot can't reach its target. A replan (#404) plans at the full rate, so a rewrite releases the armed window and puts back a limit already lowered for the slot it replaced (`plan_rate::release_for_replan`); turning the setting off clears an armed window too, while a lowered limit still goes back at the window's end. One odd read (another serial, or a limit that is neither HEM's nor the baseline) never drops a run: it takes a second read from a different frame, so a corrupt frame can't make HEM forget a baseline it still has to put back. A snapshot with no serial yet never counts as another inverter, so a restart can't drop a run. A lowered write the inverter turned away still reads as the baseline and is sent again every 2 minutes while the window is open. The limit is lowered only while charge slot 1 is running *and still reads as the plan wrote it* (`ArmedSpread::slot`, `plan_rate::planned_slot_present`): a slot 1 the user changed after Apply is never throttled, and one changed mid-window puts the limit back early. An armed window survives a start refused for want of a serial or pack size and is used up only once a run starts or is permanently refused. The Planner card's summary says the limit goes back only when the plan can apply it; otherwise it says "Would charge ... Not applied here" (`api.rs::unapplied_spread_summary`). A new run that can't be saved is dropped with its write (`plan_rate::settle_persist`). Known trade-offs: a blocker present at the moment the window opens sits the whole window out (full power is the safe direction), and the give-up notice depends on an alert channel being configured (as for #434).
- **`inverter/dispatch_charge.rs`** — battery top-up during a smart charge (issue #434), opt-in under the hold (`octopus_dispatch_charge`; `POST /api/octopus/dispatches/charge` refuses while the hold is off). While the hold is Holding a fresh smart dispatch (`running_dispatch_end` chains back-to-back ones; never a boost) it writes charge slot 1 over the dispatch's window on the inverter's clock (the inverter only charges inside a slot, and stops by itself at its end with HEM away) plus Force Charge to 100% or the charge cap's level; no rate, reserve or HR 318 write. It starts only from plain Eco once the hold's pause reads back, with no other charge owner (Force Charge, calibration, a running charge slot, Adaptive Charge, Cosy/Agile, a safety limiter), and not again in the same smart charge until SOC is 5 points below the target, saves the `ForceChargeRevert` baseline in `Settings::dispatch_charge_run` before the first write, re-sends unconfirmed settings (two fast, then every 5 min, one alert per dispatch), and restores at the dispatch's end, confirmed by a newer read (given up after 15 min with an alert); graceful shutdown restores too. Writes the inverter acknowledged during a run are taken into its baseline (`absorb`), and a manual Force Charge takes the baseline over once nothing else can fail, so Stop Charge and the restore put back the user's own settings. The Planner models it through `PlanningDispatches::charge_to_soc` → `SimHourInput::held_charge_to_soc` (not while Cosy/Agile owns the slots). Octopus bills every half-hour a *smart* dispatch touches at the off-peak rate, top-up or not: `history::OffPeakHalfHours` holds those half-hours and `RateStamp::during_smart_charges` lowers a row's import rate to the tariff's cheapest — live from `DispatchesState::off_peak_half_hours` (the fresh list plus smart charges that have since left it, kept 2 h), in a re-price from the dispatch log, and in the cheapest strategy's `cycle_cost` from the payload's `ev_dispatch_windows` (`smart`).
- **`inverter/auto_winter.rs`** — Auto Winter Mode (issue #515): keeps a cold battery warm with 30 minute charge bursts, one after another, until it has stayed at or above the Recovery Threshold for `debounce_readings` (defaults 2 °C cold, 3 °C recovery, the cold threshold above 0 °C because the BMS refuses to charge below it; the sliders are 2–10 and 3–10, enforced by `set_auto_winter` on the thresholds a request sends so older saved values still toggle). Thresholds saved by an older version are brought into range on `Settings::load` (`normalise_thresholds`: the old 8/12 defaults become 2/3, a user's own values are clamped with recovery kept above cold; the Control page applies the same pair rule). There is no Target SOC: it never writes HR 20 or HR 116, so the standard target is the cap, and on 10-slot models slot 1's own target (HR 242) when that is lower. A burst is charge slot 1 over now → now + 30 min on the inverter's clock (plus the charge flag if it was off): slot 1 is borrowed because AC-coupled, Gen 1 and Gen 2 have only one. Three-phase gets no bursts (its charge enable is HR 1112/1123, not the HR 96 a burst writes). `AutoWinterBurst` (`Settings::auto_winter_burst`) is the baseline (slot 1 and the flag as they were) and is on disk before the first write; the poll's `persist_auto_winter_run` refuses a window whose baseline couldn't be saved. Invariants: the user wins (slot 1 reading as neither HEM's window nor the baseline on two reads from different frames, `ChangedRead` as #501's `RepairReadMark`, was changed by someone else, so the baseline is dropped and never written over it, and the next poll starts afresh from what is there, so their setting becomes the one put back); a read made only of HEM's own values — the window, the window it chained from (`AutoWinterBurst::previous`, still in slot 1 until the new one reads back) and the baseline — in any mix (`SlotReads::Partial`: HR 94 and 95 go out one at a time and a batch stops at the first failed write) is sent again, three times inside a live window and then slot 1 is put back, never abandoned; the baseline lasts until slot 1 and the charge flag both read as put back; an empty serial (the first poll after a restart) waits rather than counting as another inverter, and a baseline from another serial is never written; an owner that takes slot 1 over (Force Charge, the calibration cycle, a smart-charge top-up) captures the baseline under a burst, not HEM's window (`with_baseline_slot` / `baseline_under_burst`, as Spread Charge's `with_baseline_limit`), and the burst is handed to it without a write (`hand_over`); while a burst is held `schedule_reset::hem_owns_schedule` says Auto Winter owns the schedule, so the dongle-reset watch never adopts a window; a burst restored from settings at startup resumes as a cold spell in progress (state `Warming`; from `Idle` it would read as "not warming" and put slot 1 back while the battery is still cold), and a warmed battery or a disabled mode puts it back as usual; a graceful shutdown puts slot 1 back (`restore_on_shutdown`, beside Spread Charge's) unless someone else changed it, keeping the record for the next start when the inverter doesn't answer; a restore is retried each poll; a window that never reads back is re-sent three times, then given up for 5 minutes; no burst starts over Force Charge, a safety limiter, Adaptive Charge's active period, Cosy/Agile *charging* (last poll's reading; between windows slot 1 is free), a calibration or a smart-charge top-up, while a charge slot is already running, outside plain Eco, once SOC reaches the level slot 1 stops at, or before the serial is known. `snapshot.auto_winter_active` is true only while a burst's window is live, which is what Adaptive Charge and Cosy/Agile defer to. A target saved by the version before #515 (`auto_winter_saved_*`, it used to raise the target) is put back once after the update, three attempts, then dropped.

## Registers and polling

### Gateway and slot-layout notes

Three-phase slot/target registers mirror the single-phase layout at HR 1080-1124; EMS plant-level scheduling uses HR 2040-2071. **The Gateway keeps its own copy of the schedule and battery registers and doesn't pass writes to the AIO** (issue #428, proven on hardware; it uses the single-phase layout, issue #149). HEM refuses battery controls there (`DeviceType::accepts_write`: only the clock, reboot and the HR 2071 export limit; route guard in `server/mod.rs`), its battery automations sit out (`battery_controls_reach_battery`), and the UI hides them (`deviceHasBatteryControls`). It *does* poll HR 2040-2075 for plant-level config read-back. One Gateway register layout is decoded (the one live hardware confirmed, as in GivTCP): lifetime totals are uint32 high register first and the AIO serials sit at IR 1841/1846/1851. IR(1603) holds the version's last digits as bytes (`GAAA0014` is 0x0104), so it is never a layout selector. GE Cloud UI labels slots in opposite order; the data is identical. `ControlPage.tsx` shows callout banners for the slot-naming mismatch (any 2+ slot hybrid) and legacy Gen3 firmware (ARM FW ≤ 302) where extended HR 240-299 may return stale data.

### Discharge slot handling

Saving an enabled Timed Export slot via `/api/control/discharge-slot` writes the slot + target SOC first, then arms Timed Export (`battery_power_mode=0`, `enable_discharge=1`); the button is locked until a slot exists and direct enables without a slot are rejected. Disabling the last slot while armed returns to Eco (clearing all discharge slot registers). Schedule-backup restores write slots before `enable_discharge=1`; on reconnect the poll loop repairs an externally-created armed-but-slotless state. Outside a window, the Timed Export machine and the slotless-HR59 invariant repair put the inverter back to Eco only after two reads in a row that each meet the repair's own condition (`outside_window_repair_candidate` / `should_repair_timed_export`), show the same HR27/enable/slot shape and come from different frames (`RepairReads`, by the inverter clock; a failed read breaks the run), so one bad or repeated dongle read never reaches the inverter. They log what they read (`readback_detail`), first sighting included; HEM's own unfinished stop and window exits don't wait (issue #501).

### Optional block carry-forward

Optional blocks are conditionally polled by device type: `EXTENDED_SLOTS_BLOCK` HR 240-299 (Gen3, AIO, HV Gen3, AIO Hybrid, AC-three-phase), `AC_CONFIG_BLOCK` HR 300-359 (AC-coupled, AIO, AC-three-phase), `THREE_PHASE_HIGH_CONFIG_BLOCK` HR 1000-1079 + `THREE_PHASE_CONFIG_BLOCK` HR 1080-1124 (three-phase control; the HV Gen3 and AIO Hybrid are single-phase and skip both, issue #432), `THREE_PHASE_INPUT_BLOCKS` ×7 IR 1000-1413, `GATEWAY_INPUT_BLOCKS` ×5 IR 1600-1859. When an optional block read fails, `carry_forward_optional_block_values()` preserves previous values instead of flashing zeros in the UI.

### Dongle misbehaviour tests

`npm run test:local` also runs `e2e/local-dongle-misbehaviour.spec.ts`; each test starts its own simulator + backend with `--dongle-misbehaviour <mode>`, exercising the backend's per-block retry and sanitization layers: `DropConnection` (→ Reconnecting, then reconnects), `Intermittent` (~50% zeros; stays Connected, valid data eventually), `EmptyData` (all-zero registers; snapshot shows zero power fields), `StaleData` (frozen values across polls), `GarbageData` (random u16s rejected).
